# Daily Walk · Genesis 1 — "The Creation of the Cosmos"

**Status:** Draft v1 for review
**Target length:** ~4 min (~460 words of reflection + ~120 words of verse reading at a calm ~135 wpm, plus short music breaths)
**Today's practice:** *Made in God's image.* Pick one person and quietly think those words when you see them. The home card shows this line after completion, and the next walk opens by checking in on it.
**On-screen text:** the verse text already in `lib/bible/chapters/week1-covenant.ts`, unchanged
**Voice:** warm, unhurried, conversational. A friend who knows the text well, not a preacher.

### How each beat plays

1. **RISE:** the verse rises slowly from the bottom and settles in the center.
2. **READ:** the voice reads the verse once, plainly. The text stays still.
3. **GLOW:** the highlighted phrase turns gold, and the rest of the verse dims slightly.
4. **TALK:** the conversational reflection plays. The text does not move.
5. **CLEAR:** the verse fades up and away, and the next one begins to rise.

The background painting drifts and zooms slowly the whole time.

## Beat 1 · Genesis 1:1 (≈ 0:15–0:45)

**Screen:** *In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.*
**Gold:** "God created"

**Voice (reads verse, then):**
> Notice what this first line *doesn't* do. It doesn't argue that God exists. It doesn't explain where He came from. It just opens with God already at work.
>
> And that word "created" is a special one in Hebrew: *bara*. In the Old Testament, it's only ever used with God as the one doing it. People build and shape. Only God creates like this.

---

## Beat 2 · Genesis 1:2 (≈ 0:45–1:20)

**Screen:** *Now the earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep, and the Spirit of God was hovering over the waters.*
**Gold:** "the Spirit of God was hovering"

**Voice (reads verse, then):**
> Formless. Empty. Dark. That's the starting point. Not exactly a promising scene.
>
> But look who's there. The Spirit of God, *hovering*. Later in the Bible, the same Hebrew word describes an eagle hovering over its young. It's a picture of closeness and care, not distance.
>
> So if some part of your life feels formless right now… this verse says that's exactly the kind of place God likes to begin.

---

## Beat 3 · Genesis 1:3 (≈ 1:20–1:45)

**Screen:** *And God said, 'Let there be light,' and there was light.*
**Gold:** "Let there be light"

**Voice (reads verse, then):**
> No battle. No struggle. God simply speaks… and it is.
>
> And here's something easy to miss: the sun doesn't show up until day four. Light comes first, straight from God's word. Before there was anything to see by, there was already light.

---

## Beat 4 · Genesis 1:4–5 (≈ 1:45–2:15)

**Screen:** *God saw that the light was good… And there was evening, and there was morning—the first day.*
**Gold:** "there was evening, and there was morning"

**Voice (reads verse, then):**
> Did you catch the order? Evening, *then* morning.
>
> In the Hebrew way of counting, a day begins at sundown. You start the day by resting, and the work comes after. It's a small detail, but it's a gentle reminder: the world doesn't depend on you staying awake to hold it together.

---

## Beat 5 · Genesis 1:26–27 (≈ 2:15–2:55)

**Screen:** *So God created man in His own image; in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them.*
**Gold:** "in His own image"

**Voice (reads verse 27, then):**
> In the ancient world, a king would set up statues of himself across his land, his "images", to show who was in charge.
>
> Genesis takes that idea and hands it to *everyone*. Not just kings. Every man, every woman. Each person you'll pass today carries the image of God.
>
> And yes, that includes you.

---

## Beat 6 · Genesis 1:31 (≈ 2:55–3:15)

**Screen:** *And God saw all that He had made, and behold, it was very good.*
**Gold:** "very good"

**Voice (reads verse, then):**
> Six times in this chapter, God calls what He's made "good". Only now, with people in the picture, does He say "*very* good."
>
> Before anything in the story goes wrong, this is God's first word over His world. Very good.

### Notes for review

- **Accuracy:** The claims in the reflections are mainstream and widely agreed:
  - *bara* is used only with God as the subject.
  - The verb for "hovering" (*rachaph*) appears again in Deuteronomy 32:11, for the eagle over its young.
  - Light comes on day 1 and the sun on day 4.
  - The Hebrew day runs from evening to evening.
  - Kings set up statues of themselves as "images" in the ancient Near East.
  - "Good" appears six times before "very good".
- **Deliberately left out, to stay non-denominational:**
  - the age of the earth and how to read the days
  - whether "Let Us" in verse 26 refers to the Trinity (the screen shows only verse 27)
  - creation vs. evolution
- **Verse text source:** The app's verses read like the Berean Standard Bible, which has been public domain since 2023. Confirm this before publishing, and add a translation credit to the player.
- **Next walk:** Genesis 3 follows Genesis 1 in the catalog, so the closing line names it.
