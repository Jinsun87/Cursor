## Settling in

Let yourself sink down into your bed now, as if you were lying in a little boat, rocking gently on the water.

Breathe in slowly. And breathe out slowly. Like little waves, coming in, and going out.

In. And out.

[pause]

Tonight's story happens on a lake, at night, in a wild storm. And right in the middle of the storm, someone is fast asleep. It's the story of the night Jesus told the wind and the waves to be still.

Close your eyes. Let's climb aboard.

[pause]

## Part one: the fishermen

In the land of Galilee there was a big lake called the Sea of Galilee. It was so big that from one side you could hardly see the other. And all around it were little towns, and fishing boats, and fishermen mending their nets on the shore.

Some of Jesus' closest friends were fishermen. Peter and his brother Andrew. James and his brother John. One day, Jesus had walked along the shore and called them, come, follow Me. And they left their nets, and followed Him.

[pause]

These men knew all about boats. They had been out on that lake since they were boys. They knew the wind, and the waves, and the stars.

[long pause]

## Part two: a busy day

One day, so many people came to hear Jesus that there was no room for them all on the shore. So Jesus got into a boat, and sat in it, a little way out on the water. And all the people stood along the beach, listening.

Jesus told them stories. One was about a farmer, scattering seeds. Some seeds fell on the path, and the birds gobbled them up. Some fell on rocky ground, and dried up in the sun. Some fell among thorns, and got choked. But some fell on good soil, and grew, and grew, into a great big harvest.

[pause]

And He told them another story, about the very smallest seed of all, a mustard seed. So tiny you could hardly see it, sitting on the end of your finger. But when it's planted, it grows, and grows, until it's bigger than all the other garden plants, with branches so big that the birds can come and make their nests in its shade.

That's what God's kingdom is like, Jesus said. It starts very small. But it grows into something wonderful, where everyone can come and rest.

[pause]

Jesus taught all day long. And when evening came, He was tired. He said to His friends:

> Let us cross to the other side. [Mark 4:35]

So they left the crowd on the shore, and set off across the lake in the boat, just as He was. And other boats went with them.

[long pause]

## Part three: asleep in the storm

The sun went down behind the hills. The sky turned orange, and then pink, and then deep blue. The fishermen pulled on the oars, and the sail filled with a soft evening breeze. The water lapped against the wooden sides. The lights of the little town on the shore grew smaller and smaller behind them. The boat rocked gently, gently, on the water.

And Jesus went to the back of the boat, and lay down, and put His head on a cushion. And as they sailed, He fell fast asleep.

[pause]

Picture Him there. After such a long, busy day. Curled up at the back of the boat, with the creak of the oars and the soft splash of the water all around. Sleeping peacefully, just the way you're getting ready to sleep now.

[pause]

But then, out on the lake, the wind began to blow.

It blew harder. And harder. The waves grew bigger, and bigger, splashing up over the sides of the boat. The boat was filling up with water.

The fishermen pulled at the ropes and the oars. They had been in storms before. But this one was a very bad one. And they were afraid.

[pause]

And Jesus? Still asleep. On His cushion, at the back of the boat.

[pause]

So the disciples shook Him awake, and cried:

> Teacher, don't You care that we are perishing? [Mark 4:38]

[long pause]

## Part four: be still

Jesus got up.

He looked at the wind. He looked at the waves. And He spoke to them, the way you might speak to a puppy who's being too noisy.

> Silence!… Be still! [Mark 4:39]

[long pause]

And the wind stopped. Just like that.

The waves lay down flat. The water went smooth and quiet, like glass.

> And the wind died down, and it was perfectly calm. [Mark 4:39]

[long pause]

Can you hear it? No more wind. No more crashing waves. Just the drip, drip of water from the sail. The boat rocking softly. And far above, the stars, coming out one by one.

[long pause]

## Part five: who is this?

Then Jesus turned to His friends, and asked them gently:

> Why are you so afraid?… Do you still have no faith? [Mark 4:40]

Luke tells us He asked them, “Where is your faith?” [Luke 8:25]

His friends didn't know what to say. They had seen Jesus heal people who were sick. They had heard His wonderful stories. But they had never seen anything like this. They looked at each other, with wide eyes. And they whispered:

> Who is this, that even the wind and the sea obey Him? [Mark 4:41]

[pause]

Who is this? Long before, a song in the book of Psalms had told the answer. It was about sailors caught in a storm at sea, who cried out to the LORD:

> He calmed the storm to a whisper, and the waves of the sea were hushed. [Psalm 107:29]

> They rejoiced in the silence, and He guided them to the harbor they desired. [Psalm 107:30]

Only God can calm the sea. And that night, Jesus did.

[long pause]

## Closing

Jesus wasn't worried by the storm. He could sleep right through it, because He knew His Father was looking after Him. And when His friends were afraid, He stood up, and made everything calm.

[pause]

Sometimes it can feel like there's a storm going on inside us. Worries, rushing round and round like the wind. Big feelings, crashing like waves. But Jesus is in the boat with us. And He can still say to the wind and the waves, be still.

[long pause]

Now let your breathing slow right down.

Picture the little boat on the lake, at night, after the storm. The water smooth and still. The stars reflected in it, like a second sky. The friends of Jesus lying down to rest. And Jesus, back on His cushion, asleep once more.

[pause]

Be still. All is calm.

Goodnight.

[long pause]
